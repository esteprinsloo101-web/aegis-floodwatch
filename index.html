Access

The intent is to get people off the floodplain while there is still time. A fee pays for the desk that watches every basin, not for the warning itself.

Scientific basis

AEGIS is a screening and coordination layer. It does not replace a national flood warning service or a licensed hydraulic study.

1. What actually happened in Nepal and Tibet

On 26 August 2026 an ice-rock avalanche collapsed into the Lhende River on the Nepal–Tibet divide. The debris briefly dammed the river. When that dam failed, a surge of water, ice, mud and rock ran into the Bhotekoshi–Trishuli system. This is a glacial lake outburst / debris-flood cascade, not a classic monsoon river flood.

2. Weather, hydrology, hydraulics

Ventusky has no public API. AEGIS reads the same public models through Open-Meteo and GloFAS. Hydraulics are Manning normal-depth screening, the first step HEC-RAS uses. Public CRITICAL requires two independent sources.
